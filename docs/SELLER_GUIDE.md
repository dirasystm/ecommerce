# Selling on Ecommerce: Seller Guide

For store owners and their team. Everything here happens in the **Seller Centre** (`/seller`), which you reach by signing in with your email or phone number.

## 1. Becoming a seller

1. Create a customer account (or sign in), then open **Sell on Ecommerce** (`/sell`).
2. Fill in your store and business details, add payout details (M-Pesa or bank), and upload the documents the marketplace asks for.
3. Submit. You can follow the application on the same page. Ecommerce staff review it; if anything needs changing, you are told what and can resubmit.
4. Once approved, the Seller Centre opens. The dashboard shows a short setup checklist: store description, logo, payout details and your first product.

Your documents are stored privately; only Ecommerce staff who review sellers can open them.

## 2. Your team

| Role in your store | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including payouts and payout details |
| Manager | Products, stock, orders, cases and store profile |
| Staff | Day-to-day order steps and stock |

Each person signs in with their own account. Messages (new orders, returns, cases, decisions) go to owners and managers by email and/or SMS, as each person chooses under **Notifications**. Money and account decisions are always emailed to the owner.

## 3. Products

- **Products → New:** name, category, description, price, variants (size, colour…), stock and images. Your SKUs are prefixed with your store number automatically.
- **Submit for approval:** if the marketplace reviews products, Ecommerce staff approve them or say what to change. Approved products go live on your store page and in search.
- **Stock:** update it per variant. Stock held for open orders is shown separately, so you never sell what is already promised.
- **Archive** a product to take it off sale without losing its history; restore it later.

## 4. Orders

An order can contain items from several sellers. You see and handle **your part** only.

1. **New: accept or decline.** Decline only if you cannot supply. The customer is told, and is not charged for it (or is refunded).
2. **Being prepared → Packed.**
3. **Hand over to Ecommerce.** Ecommerce collects, delivers or takes the parcel to the customer's pickup station, and marks it delivered or collected.

The dashboard counts orders at each step. Customers are messaged automatically at the steps that matter to them.

## 5. Returns and cases

- **Returns:** a customer can ask to return items within the return window. You are told when a return on your part is requested, accepted or closed. Refunds for returned items are deducted from that sale's earnings.
- **Cases:** if there is a problem with your part of an order (wrong item, damaged, not as described), the customer or Ecommerce opens a case.
  - Reply under **Cases**.
  - Earnings for that order are held until the case is closed.
  - Ecommerce decides the outcome; any refund is deducted from that sale.

## 6. Money: earnings, balances and payouts (owners)

- **How earnings work:**
  1. When a customer pays (M-Pesa verified, or cash collected), your part's sale minus Ecommerce's commission is added to **Pending**.
  2. After delivery or collection and the holding period (shown under **Earnings & payouts**; it leaves time for returns), it moves to **Available**.
- **Request a payout** of your available balance once it reaches the minimum shown. It moves to **In payout requests** until Ecommerce pays it to your payout details, then to **Paid out**. You can cancel a request that has not been paid yet.
- **Your statement** lists every movement: sales, commission, refunds, releases and payouts. Rows are never edited or deleted; corrections are new rows, so the history always adds up.
- **Reports** show sales, commission and earnings for any period.

## 7. Good to know

- **Suspended stores** can sign in and see everything, but cannot change products or orders until reactivated. **Closed stores** cannot sign in to the Seller Centre.
- Keep your payout details up to date: they are stored encrypted and shown to Ecommerce staff only when paying you.
- Questions: use the contact details on the Ecommerce **Contact** page.
